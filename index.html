// About
Every project is a scene worth directing well.

I'm Ronja, a BSc Information Systems student who treats every project like a set: nothing left to default. I'm still early in my career, but I care about the same things a director does on day one: clarity of intent, and making sure every detail earns its place.

Right now that means turning coursework, curiosity, and the occasional late night into real, shipped software, starting with tools that make studying less chaotic.

Through working student roles at Infineon Technologies, a German semiconductor company formerly part of Siemens, I've picked up two threads I keep pulling on:

01 / Workflow Automation

Building automation workflows with n8n, LiteLLM, and REST APIs to fetch data from documents and feed it into low-code platforms I developed myself on OutSystems.

02 / IT Business Analysis

Building finance and reporting dashboards, working with SAP Business Data Cloud's data lakehouse and SAP Analytics Cloud, mapping data flows, and running inventories on legacy SAP Business Warehouse systems.
